How the labels and confidence scores work

Claude’s past label judges the historical description immediately below it. “Wrong” means Claude rejected that description. It does not mean the current engine is wrong. A changed reading needs its own judgment.

The historical description is reconstructed using the archived engine where possible. Saved labels did not preserve full wording. Unverifiable descriptions show only the saved fields. Current scores are individual model probabilities when available, otherwise displayed hint/item scores. The displayed scene score may differ from the individual model probability. Missing readings have no score. My confidence is subjective, not measured accuracy.

Several labels may concern one encounter. Hint and solo labels are kept separately. Current engine comparisons match the same pattern and sentence hash; absence here does not prove that other patterns missed the act. A current “wrong” group means at least one shown reading is wrong. Only your explicit answers may become labels later; this page does not change training.

Files stay on your device. Uploads are matched by contents, so renamed files work. Cited paragraphs appear locally; story text is never published or exported.
